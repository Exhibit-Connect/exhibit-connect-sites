Privacy Policy

How we handle contact and inquiry information.

Last updated June 29, 2026. This page is a practical business policy and should be reviewed by legal counsel if formal legal advice is needed.

Who operates this site

Exhibit Connect is operated by Exhibit Connect LLC, based in New Jersey, USA. You can contact us at joseph@exhibitconnect.co.

Information we collect

When you submit a form or email us, we may collect your name, company, email address, show details, message content, and related business contact information.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to respond, evaluate fit for our services, coordinate requested business communication, maintain records, and honor opt-out or removal requests.

Service providers

This site may use hosting, email, form, analytics, or security providers such as Vercel, Google Workspace, and FormSubmit. These providers process information as needed to operate the site and business communication.

Outreach and opt-out requests

If you received a business outreach message and want no further contact, email joseph@exhibitconnect.co with the address that received the message. We will use that information to process the request.

Retention

We keep business inquiry and communication records for as long as reasonably needed for service, compliance, dispute resolution, and opt-out handling.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your inquiry information by contacting us. Some records may be retained where needed for legitimate business, legal, or suppression-list purposes.
