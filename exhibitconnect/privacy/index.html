Privacy Policy

How we handle inquiry and business contact information.

Last updated July 9, 2026.

Who operates this site

Exhibit Connect is operated by Exhibit Connect LLC, based in New Jersey, USA. Contact us at joseph@exhibitconnect.co.

Information we collect

When you submit a form or email us, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, company, email address, show details, meeting goals, and message content. Our hosting provider may also process standard request and security logs needed to serve and protect the site.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to respond, evaluate service fit, coordinate requested business communication, maintain necessary records, and honor opt-out or removal requests.

Service providers

Vercel hosts this website. FormSubmit processes website form submissions, and Google Workspace receives and stores business email. These providers process information as needed to operate the site and communication workflow.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or a user-account system. We do not set application cookies. Hosting and security providers may use essential technical mechanisms or logs to operate their services.

Outreach and opt-out requests

If you received a business outreach message and want no further contact, email joseph@exhibitconnect.co with the address that received the message. We will use that information to process the request and reflect it in future outreach handling.

Retention and your choices

We retain business inquiry, communication, and suppression records for as long as reasonably needed for service, compliance, dispute resolution, and opt-out handling. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. Some records may be retained where needed for legitimate business, legal, or suppression-list purposes.
